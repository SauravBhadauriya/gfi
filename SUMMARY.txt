🔌 BACKEND APIS INTEGRATED (3 ENDPOINTS)

1. GET /user/kyc
   └─ Returns: KYC verification status
   └─ Display: "Pending" | "Approved" | "Rejected" | "Completed"
   └─ Color: Green for Approved, Gold for others

2. GET /user/wallet
   └─ Returns: Wallet balance, total earnings, total tips
   └─ Display: Current balance in currency format

3. GET /user/earnings
   └─ Returns: Array of all earnings records
   └─ Display: Sum total formatted as ₹X,XXX.XX
