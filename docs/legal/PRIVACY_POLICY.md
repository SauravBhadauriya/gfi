# PRIVACY POLICY - GULLYFAME INDIA

**Effective Date:** 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

GullyFame India ("we," "us," "our," or the "Company") operates the GullyFame India mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable rules, and other applicable privacy, cybersecurity and intermediary requirements.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we retain it, the choices and rights available to you, and how you can contact us.

---

## 2. SCOPE

This Policy applies to personal data processed through the Platform, including the mobile application, websites, APIs, integrations, creator tools, competitions, payments, support channels and related services.

Where a feature has a separate privacy notice or specific terms, that notice will apply together with this Policy.

---

## 3. DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Policy:

- **"Personal Data"** means data about an identifiable individual, as defined by applicable law.
- **"Data Principal"** means the individual to whom the personal data relates.
- **"Consent"** means a lawful, informed, specific and unambiguous indication of the individual's wishes.
- **"User Content"** means photos, videos, audio, text, comments, messages and other material submitted or shared through the Platform.

---

## 4. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect only information reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy and as permitted by applicable law.

### 4.1 Information You Provide

- **Identity data:** name, date of birth, gender and profile photograph
- **Contact data:** mobile number, email address and, where required, address/city/state/PIN code
- **Account data:** username, password and account-security information
- **User Content:** photos, videos, audio, text posts, comments, messages and other content you upload or share
- **Communications:** support requests, feedback, surveys and correspondence
- **Payment/KYC data:** bank account details, IFSC, UPI ID, payment information and, where legally/operationally necessary, government identification such as PAN or other permitted KYC information

### 4.2 Sensitive/High-Risk Information

Where the Platform uses biometric authentication or processes other information that is specially regulated or otherwise high-risk, we will provide the required notice and obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis where permitted.

### 4.3 Information Collected Automatically

We may collect device and technical information such as device model, operating-system version, app version, identifiers permitted by law, IP address, logs, crash reports, performance information, timestamps and security signals.

We may collect approximate or precise location only where enabled by you, required for a feature, or otherwise permitted by law, if needed.

### 4.4 Information From Third Parties

We may receive information from connected accounts, payment providers, KYC providers, fraud-prevention services, analytics providers, business partners and other lawful sources, subject to applicable law and the permissions or consents required.

## 5. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

We may process personal data to:

- (a) Create and manage accounts
- (b) Authenticate users and recover accounts
- (c) Provide, maintain and improve the Platform
- (d) Personalise feeds, recommendations and features
- (e) Enable creator tools, competitions, rewards, tipping, commerce and payouts
- (f) Process payments, refunds, KYC and tax-related requirements
- (g) Communicate service updates, security alerts and support responses
- (h) Send marketing or promotional communications where required with consent or another lawful basis
- (i) Detect fraud, spam, abuse, manipulation and security threats
- (j) Moderate and protect the Platform and its community
- (k) Analyse usage, troubleshoot and develop products
- (l) Comply with applicable law, court orders and lawful government requests

## 6. LAWFUL BASIS, NOTICE AND CONSENT

We process personal data on the basis permitted by applicable law, including consent where required, specified legitimate uses/lawful purposes where available, compliance with legal obligations, and other permitted grounds.

Where consent is required, we will provide an appropriate notice and seek consent in a manner required by law. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal may affect features that require that processing.

Where the Platform operates competitions or rewards, we may process account, content, engagement, voting, judging, referral, payment and verification information to administer the competition, detect manipulation, verify eligibility, determine winners, make payouts and handle disputes.

## 11. AI, DEEPFAKES AND SYNTHETIC CONTENT

GullyFame may process content and technical signals to identify or manage AI-generated, synthetically generated or materially manipulated content where required for safety, transparency, legal compliance or Platform integrity.

Users must comply with applicable requirements concerning disclosure, labelling or other treatment of synthetically generated information as detailed in the FAQs. Content that impersonates a real person, deceives users, facilitates fraud, or otherwise violates applicable law or Platform rules may be labelled, restricted or removed.

Any violation will lead to immediate content deletion, temporary account suspension, or permanent termination, along with forfeiture of account rights.

## 12. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We may share personal data with:

- (a) Hosting, cloud, analytics, moderation, customer-support, payment, KYC and security service providers acting on our behalf
- (b) Affiliates where permitted and necessary for operations
- (c) Business partners for features you choose to use, subject to applicable requirements
- (d) Advertising or measurement providers as permitted by law
- (e) Law-enforcement, regulators, courts or government authorities where legally required or permitted

We require appropriate contractual, technical or organisational safeguards from relevant processors/service providers where required. We do not sell personal data in the ordinary course of business.

## 13. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, for the operation and security of the Platform, and for applicable legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution and enforcement requirements.

Retention varies by category:
- Account data may be retained while an account is active and for a limited period thereafter
- Payment/KYC and tax records may be retained for periods required by law
- Security and fraud logs may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to protect the Platform
- Support or dispute records may be retained for a reasonable period to resolve and evidence the matter

When retention is no longer necessary and no legal exception applies, we will delete or anonymise the relevant personal data.

## 14. DATA DELETION AND ACCOUNT CLOSURE

Where the Platform provides account deletion, you may initiate deletion through the account/settings process or by contacting us through the privacy contact below.

Deletion may not immediately remove information that must be retained by law or is required for security, fraud prevention, financial records, disputes or legal claims.

Public content, competition records, transaction records or information already lawfully shared with third parties may be subject to separate deletion, retention or archival rules.
