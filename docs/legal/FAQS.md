## 5. What are the rules regarding AI-Generated Content, Deepfakes, and Synthetic Media?

GullyFame India values authenticity and transparency:

### Disclosure & Labelling
Any content created, modified, or enhanced using Artificial Intelligence (AI), deepfake technology, synthetic voice generation, or manipulated media must be clearly labelled and disclosed prior to publishing.

### Prohibited Synthetic Content
Users are strictly prohibited from uploading AI-generated media designed to:
- Deceive or impersonate real individuals without consent
- Defame, spread misinformation, or create non-consensual explicit images
- Facilitate fraud or identity theft

### Enforcement
Unlabelled or harmful synthetic content will be removed immediately upon detection or reporting, and offending accounts may face permanent termination.

### Deepfake Removal Timeline
Non-consensual explicit content, severe identity theft, or government removal orders are reviewed immediately and can be taken down within a strict **3-hour window**.

### How to Report AI Impersonation
There is a simple step-by-step reporting guide for using the in-app report button under "Impersonation / AI Deepfake" which provides the Grievance Officer's direct contact information.

### Appeal Process for False Takedowns
There is an appeal process to escalate disputes to the Grievance Appellate Committee (GAC).
